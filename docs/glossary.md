# Glossary

## Domain terms

- **Blank session:** fishing session without catches. It is a valid outcome and provides information for evaluating recommendations.
- **Confidence:** measure separate from the score that expresses the quality, freshness, completeness, and suitability of available data.
- **Conditions:** environmental observations or forecasts relevant to a fishing opportunity.
- **Horizon:** future interval covered by recommendations; initially 72 hours.
- **Fishing opportunity:** time-specific combination of spot, species, technique, conditions, score, confidence, explanation, and safety status.
- **Recommendation:** guidance on where, when, and how to fish, accompanied by factors, confidence, and limitations. It never promises catches.
- **Score:** explainable rating from 0 to 100 for the relative potential of the conditions. It is not a catch probability.
- **Session:** recorded fishing activity, with or without catches, which can be linked to a recommendation.
- **Spot:** fishing area with local context. It does not imply that its exact coordinates are public or verified.
- **Shore spinning:** initial fishing method based on casting and retrieving artificial lures from the shore.
- **Vertical slice:** small increment covering the entire experience needed to validate a product outcome.

## Documentation states

- **`decided`:** current accepted decision, although it may not yet be implemented.
- **`proposed`:** proposed direction pending confirmation.
- **`future`:** outside the immediate scope and reserved for a later phase.
- **`needs-validation`:** hypothesis, data, or decision that requires evidence or a current source.
- **`not-integrated`:** identified source without technical integration.
