# Regulatory research

**Status: `needs-validation`; no legal advice.**

`alerts.fishing` must refer to current official sources and must not present itself as a substitute for authorities, licenses, or external services.

## Initial references

- [PescaREC](https://www.mapa.gob.es/es/pesca/temas/pesca-maritima-de-recreo/pesca-rec/) — `not-integrated`; official external reference on recreational marine fishing.
- [SIPMAR Xunta — Especies](https://pescreativa.xunta.gal/es/especies) — `not-integrated`; official external reference for Galicia.

Before using information from either source, its license, attribution, terms of use, currency, jurisdiction, and update procedure will be verified.

## Information to verify

- applicable licenses and methods;
- current species, closed seasons, minimum sizes, quotas, and restrictions;
- protected or restricted-access areas;
- rules specific to a place, fishing method, or season;
- authority and effective date of each rule;
- handling of changes, discrepancies, and source unavailability.

## Presentation rules

- Display the official source and consultation or effective date.
- Distinguish an informational alert from a legal determination.
- Do not claim that an activity is legal because data is absent.
- In the event of conflict or a missing update, warn the user and direct them to the official source.
- Do not rely exclusively on an LLM to interpret or apply rules.
